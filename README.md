## ✨ What is JoinDev?

**JoinDev** is a free Discord-based server growth platform. It connects server owners through a **Join 4 Join** network — you join other servers, earn **JoinCoins**, and spend them to attract real members to your own community.

No paywalls. No premium tiers. No fake members. Just real growth.

## 🚀 How It Works

| Step | What Happens |
| :---: | :--- |
| **1** | **Authorize the bot** — Click the link on our site and approve JoinDev through Discord's secure OAuth2 flow. |
| **2** | **Install to your servers** — Pick which of your servers you want to grow. |
| **3** | **Start farming** — Join other servers, earn JoinCoins, and spend them to grow your own. |

---

## 🪙 What Are JoinCoins?

**1 JoinCoin = 1 member.**

- Get **10 free JoinCoins** just for authorizing.
- Run `/daily` in DMs for **3 coins every 24 hours**.
- Keep a streak alive and earn **+1 bonus coin per consecutive day**.
- Spend coins with `/buy_members` or farm with `/auto_join`.

---

## 🛡️ Safety & Transparency

- ✅ **No message reading** — the bot never reads your messages or DMs
- ✅ **Discord-verified joins** — every member is real, authenticated through Discord
- ✅ **Revocable anytime** — remove the bot or revoke access from your Discord settings
- ✅ **Auto-shutdown protection** — if the bot token is ever compromised, the service shuts itself down automatically

---

## 📋 Commands

| Command | What It Does |
| :--- | :--- |
| `/daily` | Claim your daily JoinCoins and build your streak |
| `/balance` | Check your current JoinCoin balance |
| `/auto_join` | Farm JoinCoins by joining servers (or use `max`) |
| `/buy_members` | Spend JoinCoins to attract members to your server |
| `/cancel_joinr` | Stop all pending joins immediately |

## 🔒 Security Notice

This repository is the **backend service** for JoinDev. It handles OAuth2 token exchange, user storage, and Discord bot logic.

**All secrets are managed through environment variables** — no tokens, client secrets, or database credentials are ever committed to this repository.
